## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmosphere of intellectual distinction, institutional permanence, and warm community accessibility. Built for a forward-thinking K–12 academy, it balances historic collegiate dignity with contemporary digital clarity.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface must inspire deep institutional confidence in prospective parents, rigorous clarity for enrolled families, and pride among students and faculty. The visual tone is scholarly yet never cold; stately yet welcoming. It evokes the stability of heritage stone architecture paired with the precision of modern scholarship.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Editorial & Structured Academic
The visual architecture blends classical editorial layout with structured digital utility:
- **Refined Proportion:** Editorial vertical rhythm, generous line lengths, and deliberate whitespace communicate restraint and prestige.
- **Micro-Contrast & Precision:** Crisp keylines, understated borders, and subtle structural grids replace dramatic drop shadows or trendy surface blurs.
- **Restrained Focus:** High-contrast navy and parchment-tinted surfaces form the foundational canvas, while rich amber accents are reserved for actionable emphasis, achievements, and institutional milestones.

## Colors

The palette grounds the academic environment in authoritative deep blues, complemented by warm gold/amber accents that signal distinction and attention.

### Color Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#0F294A` & `#1A365D`):** Primary anchors all main navigational structures, typographic anchors, primary calls-to-action, and key visual containers. It represents heritage, truth, and scholarly rigor.
- **Secondary Academic Amber (`#D99B26`):** The secondary hue is reserved for merit badges, highlights, secondary buttons, active indicator pills, and key interactive moments. It evokes parchment warmth, academic honors, and optimism.
- **Tertiary Slate Navy (`#1A365D`):** Provides tonal variation for hover states, focused sub-headers, and intermediate structural cards.
- **Slate Neutrals (`#475569` base):** Ranging from deep charcoal body text (`#1E293B`) to muted metadata (`#64748B`), borders (`#E2E8F0`), and tinted wash backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas is clean, pure white to ensure maximum contrast and legible academic reading.

### Functional States & Contrast
Every core interactive color pairing meets strict WCAG AA/AAA standards (e.g., `#0F294A` over white exceeds a 13:1 contrast ratio). The gold accent is strictly applied against dark navy backgrounds or as an outline/fill paired with dark charcoal text to avoid low-contrast readability failures.

## Typography

Typography bridges time-honored academic literature with clear administrative and instructional utility.

### Type Pairings
- **Headings (Merriweather):** Chosen for its literary authority, structural legibility, and humanist warmth. It evokes published journals, diplomas, and library architecture. Display titles use bold weights, while secondary section titles leverage its classic serif italics or regular cuts.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A contemporary grotesque sans-serif with subtle geometric curves. It ensures rapid scanability for student dashboards, schedules, tuition portals, and grade books.

### Typographic Discipline
- **Hierarchy:** Strict two-level jump between heading tags to maintain visual cadence.
- **Editorial Eyebrows:** Category indicators and section markers use `label-sm` or `label-md` set in uppercase with tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) in deep slate or amber.
- **Line Length:** Body paragraphs are constrained to a maximum of 68 characters per line (`max-w-prose`) to preserve optimal reading fatigue limits.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial logic enforces a calm, disciplined structure inspired by classical proportions and structured editorial layouts.

### Grid & Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum outer margin of 48px (`3rem`). Maximum content container spans 1280px to prevent horizontal sprawl.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px (`2rem`) outer margins. Multi-column cards collapse from 4 columns to 2.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margins. All horizontal multi-card blocks stack linearly into single-column modules.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere to generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` to `4rem`) to prevent cognitive clutter. Academic calendars, directories, and portals employ compact internal padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`), balancing informational density with administrative legibility.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain institutional credibility and a timeless feel, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows, neon glows, or high-blur glass surfaces. Depth is established through structural layering and soft, low-intensity ambient shadows.

### Atmospheric Planes
1. **Base Foundation (Level 0):** Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) or subtle slate wash (`#F8FAFC`). Surfaces remain flat with crisp 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`).
2. **Elevated Academic Cards (Level 1):** Resting surfaces (course cards, faculty directory tiles, news articles) use an ultra-soft navy-tinted ambient shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.04), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.03);` coupled with a hairline border (`#EDF2F7`).
3. **Interactive & Floating Panels (Level 2):** Hover states, contextual dropdowns, and flyout navigation menus lift with increased vertical displacement:
   `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.04);`
4. **Modal Dialogs & Critical Alerts (Level 3):** Centered alerts and admissions submission modals use deep scrim shading (`rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.6)`) to focus absolute attention on crucial institutional decisions.

## Shapes

The geometric personality aligns with level `1` (Soft), utilizing refined, subtle corner radii that convey structure, precision, and stability without appearing harsh or hyper-playful.

### Geometry Rules
- **Standard UI Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** 4px (`0.25rem`) border radius. This creates clean, architectural contours consistent with traditional parchment documents and formal institutional collateral.
- **Structural Containers (Cards, Modals, Callout Panels):** 8px (`0.5rem`) border radius (`rounded-lg`). Ensures broad surfaces soften without losing their structural authority.
- **Accents & Pills:** Informational tags (e.g., "Honor Roll", "Admissions Open") may use pill-shapes to denote transient or status-oriented states while all foundational structural boundaries remain softly squared.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid collegiate navy background (`#0F294A`), crisp white text, 4px radius, 12px vertical / 24px horizontal padding. Hover shifts smoothly to tertiary navy (`#1A365D`) with a 1px vertical lift.
- **Secondary Distinction:** Rich warm amber fill (`#D99B26`), dark navy text (`#0F294A`), bold weight for high legibility. Used for critical conversion paths like "Apply Now" or "Schedule Tour".
- **Tertiary / Outline:** 1.5px solid border in navy (`#0F294A`) or slate (`#CBD5E1`) with transparent fill and navy text.

### Cards
- **Curriculum & Program Cards:** Built on a white canvas with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and Level 1 elevation. Includes an optional 3px top accent border in primary navy or secondary amber to signify division level (Lower School, Middle School, Upper School).
- **Interactive Behavior:** On hover, cards transition their border color to slate navy (`#1A365D`) and apply Level 2 elevation with a 2px upward translation.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Small caps `label-sm` with 4px radius and soft contextual washes (e.g., Amber tint `#FEF3C7` with `#92400E` text for pending reviews; Slate wash `#F1F5F9` with `#334155` text for general terms).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** Crisp 1px slate border (`#CBD5E1`), 4px corner radius, standard 16px body typography. Focus ring uses a double-ring mechanism: 1px internal navy border accompanied by a 3px soft amber or navy halo (`rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Navy fill on selected state with clean white iconography.

### Academic-Specific Modules
- **Grade & Transcript Tables:** Alternating rows using subtle slate washes (`#F8FAFC`), explicit 1px horizontal borders, monospaced numerals for numeric scores, and bold serif row headings.
- **Faculty & Staff Directory Block:** Square or 4px-radius portrait avatars paired with serif staff names, sans-serif credentials, and slate department badges.
