Upper School Spotlight

The Harkness Round Table Tradition

Upper division humanities seminars are conducted entirely at oval tables of no more than 14 scholars, cultivating civil discourse, rhetorical mastery, and rapid peer synthesis.

GRADES 6–8 Inquiry, Self-Advocacy & Empirical Reasoning

Navigating Complexity with Curiosity

The Middle School bridge is designed around developmental psychology: cultivating critical autonomy while safeguarding collaborative empathy. Students rotate through intensive laboratory blocks, explore world languages, and develop structured argumentative writing across every discipline.
