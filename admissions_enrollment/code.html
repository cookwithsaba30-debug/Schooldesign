Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the daily cadence, support structures, and policies shaping life at St. Jude Academy.

What are the typical class sizes and student-teacher ratios? expand_more
In the Lower School (K–4), classes are strictly capped at 14 students with a lead faculty member and a dedicated associate teacher in every room. In Middle and Upper Divisions, seminars and laboratory sections maintain an average of 14 scholars (maximum ceiling of 16), ensuring that every student presents, defends coursework, and receives extensive written feedback weekly.
Does St. Jude Academy accept mid-year transfer students? expand_more
Yes, rolling mid-year admissions are considered primarily for relocating families subject to seat availability in the desired grade level. Mid-year applicants follow the same comprehensive evaluation pathway, with expedited committee reviews convened within 10 school days of receipt of all transcripts and recommendations.
What bus transportation routes and zones are provided? expand_more
We operate six dedicated private chartered routes serving Westover Hills, Brookline, Newton, Wellesley, Cambridge, and South End Boston. Buses are equipped with GPS tracking accessible through our Parent Mobile Portal, Wi-Fi connectivity for academic study, and supervised by Academy transport monitors.
What is the school uniform dress code policy? expand_more
Scholars wear our traditional navy and heather-gray uniform provided through our official outfitter, Dennis Uniform. The dress code instills a sense of shared community purpose and equality. Formal dress uniform (blazer, school tie, and crest insignia) is required on Wednesdays for All-School Convocation and guest lecture assemblies.
What ESL and Academic Support services are available? expand_more
Our Academic Learning Center provides specialized linguistic enrichment for bilingual scholars and targeted executive functioning coaching. While we expect candidates to possess working English proficiency upon entry, dedicated ESL specialists provide targeted analytical writing and rhetoric seminars to ensure seamless scholarly integration.
